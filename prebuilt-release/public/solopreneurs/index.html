The 8 Workflows That Eat Your Week

1Enquiry Response & Lead Capture
Right now: Gets a response when you check your phone. By then they've contacted 3 other coaches.
Automated: Instant response within 90 seconds, qualifying questions, CRM record, booking link.
2Discovery Call Booking
Right now: Back-and-forth DMs to find a time.
Automated: Prospect qualifies → sees real-time availability → books directly → confirmation + prep email + reminders.
3Client Onboarding
Right now: Manually send welcome email, share Drive folder, explain process on first call.
Automated: Client signs → welcome sequence, intake form, prep guide, scheduling link all sent automatically.
4Session Follow-Up
Right now: Send notes when you remember. Sometimes you forget.
Automated: Session ends → follow-up sent within 2 hours with summary, action items, resources, next booking link.
5Social Media Content Repurposing
Right now: Creating for 4 platforms takes 10+ hrs/week.
Automated: Record one video → AI extracts clips → formats for Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts → scheduled.
6Invoice & Payment Collection
Right now: Create invoices manually, sometimes late, sometimes forgotten.
Automated: Package purchased/session completed → invoice sent → reminders at 3/7/14 days.
7Testimonial & Review Collection
Right now: Occasionally ask happy clients. Most say yes but never follow through.
Automated: Client hits milestone → request sent with direct link → follow-up if not completed.
8Re-Engagement & Upsell
Right now: Past clients disappear. Nobody reaches out.
Automated: 30/60/90-day sequence checking in and offering next program.

Three Growth Levers Automation Unlocks for Solopreneurs

When operational weight lifts, three distinct growth paths open up - none of which require you to work more hours.

01💰
More 1:1 Clients
+$50,000–60,000/yr

Reducing 15 hrs of admin to 3-5 hrs recovers 10-12 hrs/week - enough for 5-6 more client sessions. At $200/session: $1,000-$1,200/week additional capacity, without working longer days.

02👥
Launch Group Programs
Scale beyond 1:1

The time you recover is for building the group program or course you've been putting off. Automation handles enrolment, onboarding, and follow-up for group programs automatically.

03🔄
Build a Content Engine
Create once, distribute everywhere

When repurposing is automated, your audience grows → pipeline grows → sessions fill. The flywheel turns because you're not manually managing every piece of content.
